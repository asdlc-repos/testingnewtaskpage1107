# Overview

An internal leave management application where WSO2 employees can request time off and their managers can review and respond to those requests.

# Personas

- Employee — requests leave and checks their balance
- Manager — reviews and approves or rejects leave requests from their team

# Features

- An employee submits a leave request by choosing dates and a leave type.
- Employees see their current leave balance for each type.
- Managers view pending requests from their direct reports.
- A manager approves or rejects a request with an optional comment.
- Employees see the status of their submitted requests — pending, approved, or rejected.
